Quran data used in this app
- Arabic (Uthmani) text: The Noble Qur'an Encyclopedia, https://quranenc.com
- English transliteration and the English translation (Saheeh International, by Umm Muhammad): Tanzil, https://tanzil.net
- Compiled as JSON by quran-json (Risan Bagja Pradana), https://github.com/risan/quran-json, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
  The files in this folder are a reformatting of that data (the text itself is unchanged) and are shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Tanzil terms: the text may be used with the source clearly indicated and a link to https://tanzil.net, and must not be changed.
